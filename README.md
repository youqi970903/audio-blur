# Audio Blur

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文)

## English

I like having a familiar streamer on while I write. It makes working alone feel a little less quiet. So I’d turn the volume down, go back to my draft, and then catch one sentence. A minute later, I’d be listening to the stream with my hands still on the keyboard.

I made Audio Blur for that exact moment. It softens the sound of the current Chrome tab so I can keep a bit of that familiar voice in the room and get back to what I was writing.

You can adjust how muffled it sounds while it plays. When you actually want to hear what they’re saying, flip the switch off to bring back the original sound.

### Try it

1. Download and unzip [Audio Blur v1.0.1](./Audio_Blur_v1.0.1.zip).
2. In Chrome 116 or later, open `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked**, and select the folder containing `manifest.json`.
4. Play a video or livestream, click the Audio Blur icon, and turn it on for that tab. Move the slider until the sound feels right.

## 中文

我写东西的时候，喜欢开着一个熟悉的主播。一个人坐在电脑前，听着有人说话，就没那么安静了。可每次把音量调低、回去写自己的东西，只要刚好听清一句，我就会忍不住接着听。手还放在键盘上，文档里的光标已经闪了半天。

所以我做了 Audio Blur。它能把当前 Chrome 标签页的声音变模糊，让我留一点熟悉的人声在背景里，接着写刚才那句话。

你可以边听边调，找到自己觉得舒服的模糊程度。真想听听主播在说什么，关掉开关就能听回原声。

### 怎么试

1. 下载并解压 [Audio Blur v1.0.1](./Audio_Blur_v1.0.1.zip)。
2. 在 Chrome 116 或更新版本中打开 `chrome://extensions`。
3. 开启**开发者模式**，点击**加载已解压的扩展程序**，选中包含 `manifest.json` 的文件夹。
4. 播放视频或直播，点击 Audio Blur 图标，为当前标签页打开开关，再把滑块调到自己喜欢的位置。
